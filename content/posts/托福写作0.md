### 关于国际关系的一篇

### 关于 UN 的一篇

问题：We have been examining the role of international organizations such as the United Nations in maintaining international order. Some people believe these organizations are essential for global peace and security, while others believe they are ineffective and should be reformed. What role do you think international organizations should play in promoting global peace and security?

---

文章：In response to the discussion, I agree with Andrew that the United Nations plays a vital role in **upholding an ordered society**. However, I also share Claire’s concern that existing systems often struggle with **recurring problems** like geopolitical tensions and the plight of **refugees**.

From my perspective, international organizations must **place a high priority on** **safeguarding our security** by **regulating and overseeing** global conflicts. It is **short-sighted** to believe that global stability should rely solely on major powers. Instead, the international community would **be well-advised to** **promote equality** by encouraging smaller nations to form unions that can **counteract** unilateral military influence. While ensuring absolute justice is an **arduous process**, fostering a more **tolerant and open-minded** framework will ensure that these organizations remain **very rewarding** for the entire global community.

-----

## 写作框架：第一次写新托福作文

新托福作文可以先搭出一个清晰、稳定的两段式框架，全文写到 **150 词左右**通常就已经足够。

第一句：直接表态你支持谁，或者你在两人的争论中持什么立场。

- **话术**：*I clear agree with Andrew's point that...* 或 *While Claire raises a valid concern about X, I believe Y is more critical.*

第二句可以回复一下第二个人的想法

那么这个就是第一段了。

第二段开篇第一句表明自己的核心论点（From my perspective）

然后可以写一句论证

写一句Counter

再写一个解决方法（创新点）

然后揆诸当下

150个词左右 其实是差不多

## 第一段：回应讨论区（以他人观点作为切入点）

> **In response to the discussion, I agree with Andrew that the United Nations plays a vital role in upholding an ordered society.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**明确表态。** 快速挂钩讨论区，赞同 Andrew。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`upholding an ordered society`（维持有序社会），非常地道的学术表达，比写 maintain a stable society 更高级。
>
> **However, I also share Claire’s concern that existing systems often struggle with recurring problems like geopolitical tensions and the plight of refugees.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**展现批判性思维。** 通过转折，吸收 Claire 的合理担忧，让文章显得客观、不偏激。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`recurring problems`（反复出现的问题）、`geopolitical tensions`（地缘政治紧张局势）、`plight of refugees`（难民困境）。这三个词直接拉高了文章的学术内涵。

------

## 第二段：独立立论区（全力阐述自己的独立贡献）

> **From my perspective, international organizations must place a high priority on safeguarding our security by regulating and overseeing global conflicts.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**提出个人总论点。** 明确指出国际组织未来的核心首要任务。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`place a high priority on`（高度重视）、`safeguarding`（捍卫/保护）、`regulating and overseeing`（规范与监督）。
>
> **It is short-sighted to believe that global stability should rely solely on major powers.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**反向论证（驳论）。** 批判错误观念，为自己接下来的“小国结盟”方案开辟逻辑空间。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`short-sighted`（目光短浅的）、`rely solely on`（完全依赖）。
>
> **Instead, the international community would be well-advised to promote equality by encouraging smaller nations to form unions that can counteract unilateral military influence.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**提出具体解决方案（核心创新点）。** 解释如何解决大国垄断的问题。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`be well-advised to`（被建议/明智之举）、`counteract`（抵消/抗衡）、`unilateral military influence`（单边军事影响力，极佳的政治学词汇）。
>
> **While ensuring absolute justice is an arduous process, fostering a more tolerant and open-minded framework will ensure that these organizations remain very rewarding for the entire global community.**
>
> - 【**框架功能**】：**总结升华。** 承认现实局限，同时展望积极的未来。
> - 【**词汇亮点**】：`arduous process`（艰难的过程）、`fostering`（培养/孕育）、`tolerant and open-minded`（包容与开放的）、`very rewarding`（非常有益的/值得的）。
