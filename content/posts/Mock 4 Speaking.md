教育是否应该转向培养学生的解决问题能力？

我的表达：

I strongly support this argument. The first reason is that we are now living in the AI era, which is developing very fast. As technology develops rapidly, new problems also come very fast. In order to solve these new problems, children need to be very resilient, smart, and prepared for these changes. We shouldn't fear these changes. 替换后的表达

I am a **firm believer** in this argument. We are currently living in an era of **artificial intelligence**, where **scientific breakthroughs** are **speeding up the pace of** social change. As technology **breaks new ground** at an incredible rate, **recurring problems** emerge, often making adaptation an **arduous process**.

To effectively tackle these challenges, **tomorrow's students** must learn to **use lateral thinking** and enhance their **problem-solving ability**. Rather than being **shrouded in secrecy** or fear, children should be encouraged to **welcome** these advancements **with open arms**. Therefore, fostering these qualities should be one of the primary **aims of education**, and such training must be integrated into the **core curriculum** from as early as primary school to ensure they grow into **well-rounded** and **well-adjusted adults**.





原题：Thank you for agreeing to participate. I'd like to ask you some questions about childhood education. First, did you enjoy your school experience as a child?

我的表达：

I enjoyed my education experience as a child very much. First of all, the teachers in my primary school and kindergarten were very kind and really took care of me; I felt a lot of love and care from them. Secondly, I also learned many life skills that a child needs from them. I believe they prepared me very well to be a better student in junior high school.

替换后

I look back on my early **education** as a **very rewarding** experience. During my time in kindergarten and **primary school**, I was fortunate enough to have teachers who were true **mentors**. They effectively **took me under their wing**, providing a level of care that fostered a strong **sense of belonging**.

Beyond basic academics, they helped me **acquire knowledge** and essential **problem-solving abilities** that every child needs. I am a **firm believer** that their guidance **formed the basis of** my future **academic attainment**, ensuring I was **well-prepared** to become a **well-rounded** student in junior high school.

### **核心地道表达解析（复习重点）**

1. **use lateral thinking (使用发散性思维):** 比smart更有力气
2. **form the basis of (为...下基础):** 这是描述“基础教育”或“早期经历”对未来影响时的万能句型，指为后续事物的持续发展提供想法或行动支撑。
3. **academic attainment (学业成就):** 常用
4. **be very rewarding (很有回报):** 就是“好”
5. **take someone under sb.'s wing (悉心关照某人):** 老师关爱学生的表达
6. **scientific breakthroughs (科学突破)**
